Ossiframe Doctor and Ossiframe Deploy for macOS on Apple Silicon.

- Install: see [docs/install.md](https://github.com/shojibur/Ossiframe/blob/main/docs/install.md). Check the download against `SHA256SUMS` first.
- Known limitations: see [docs/known-limitations.md](https://github.com/shojibur/Ossiframe/blob/main/docs/known-limitations.md).
- Both apps are local-first. They need no account and no Ossiframe backend.

Assets:
- `Ossiframe-Doctor_<version>_aarch64.dmg` and `.app.zip`: read-only server discovery and audit.
- `Ossiframe-Deploy_<version>_aarch64.dmg` and `.app.zip`: provisioning and application deployment.
- `SHA256SUMS`: SHA-256 checksums of every asset.
